With xmas coming up I don't think I'm going to have much time to revise before the new year so I might try and get the reconnaissance and reporting module done. I've got a European road trip coming up next week so I should probably shun my shiny new Kindle for the course materials.

If there is anyone reading this who as done the GPEN exam and would like to share some tips on areas to concentrate on I would defiantly welcome the input.
